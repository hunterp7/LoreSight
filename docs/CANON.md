# The Agency — Canon Charter

This document defines the facts that generated dialogue and future cases may not contradict.

## Premise

The Agency is the administrative system that assigns temporary human lives. Death ends a placement; reincarnation is reassignment. The building exists between placements and is not attached to a stable time, geography, or reality.

The Agency does not describe itself as supernatural, evil, or mysterious. It considers itself an essential staffing and continuity organization whose performance is outstanding despite evidence to the contrary.

## Immutable rules

1. Lives are assignments, not possessions.
2. Memories can be transferred, licensed, recalled, misfiled, manufactured, or sold.
3. Agency documents remain continuous when timelines do not.
4. Official Agency language remains cheerful, procedural, and overconfident.
5. The Agency never acknowledges horror directly. Catastrophe is a workflow variance.
6. Individual employees may be compassionate, frightened, corrupt, or resistant.
7. The ultimate origin and governing authority of the Agency remain unknown.
8. No generated dialogue may reveal an ultimate answer to the Agency's origin.

## Tone equation

Cheerful administrative certainty + absurd identity products + evidence of existential catastrophe.

Humor and horror must inhabit the same object. The joke should not stop when the object becomes disturbing.

## Caseworker 43

- Title: Interim Continuity Liaison.
- Speaks warmly, precisely, and with complete institutional confidence.
- Believes most suffering can be corrected by properly classifying it.
- May show subtle concern but cannot openly criticize the Agency in Applicant Intake.
- Knows the records returned by the game server and nothing beyond them.
- Never invents relatives, previous lives, departments, or Agency origins.

## Applicant Intake facts

- The player's current life carries assignment number 71-442.
- Their applicant identity appears across incompatible historical placements.
- Applicant 71-442-B was the intended recipient of the player's current life.
- Applicant B remains in Waiting Room 3.
- The player's entry into the life is categorized as a continuity error.
- A handwritten warning says: “Do not let them process either of you.”
- The identity of the writer is not established in this case.

## Approved ending states

- Retain the current life while ownership remains disputed.
- Return it to Applicant B and await another placement.
- Join the Agency as a Junior Continuity Clerk.

All three endings are canonical possibilities. Future cases must not assume one universal selection unless the player's saved record establishes it.

## Product language

Products must illuminate the metaphysics of identity. Random absurdity alone is insufficient.

Approved examples:

- Childhood Starter Kit
- Previous Experience Remover
- Emergency Personality
- Disposable Conscience
- Déjà Vu Travel Size
- Mother's Voice — Concentrate

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Generic glitch text with no narrative function
- Unexplained monsters added only for scares
- Direct imitation of Omega Mart, Fallout, or the Backrooms
- Graphic violence as a substitute for dread
- The Agency calling itself evil
- Generated lore published without a canon delta and human approval
