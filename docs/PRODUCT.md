# Applicant Intake — Product Definition

## One-sentence promise

Dispute your death, audit the bureaucracy that assigned your identity, and decide who is entitled to your life.

## Release scope

Applicant Intake is a complete, free, general-audience ChatGPT-native narrative game. It is not a trial and contains no paid digital upsell.

Target play time: 20–30 minutes.

## Player fantasy

I am auditing the institution that manufactured my identity.

## Primary verbs

- Answer
- Lie
- Refuse
- Examine
- Cross-reference
- Confront
- Choose

## Core loop

1. Converse with Caseworker 43.
2. Receive a canonical discrepancy.
3. Open the Agency terminal.
4. Examine a record or product.
5. Bring the contradiction back into conversation.
6. Unlock a deeper record.
7. Choose a final disposition.

## Story beats

1. Mortality review: the Agency claims the player is between lives.
2. Memory review: the player disputes how childhood memories establish identity.
3. Evidence release: three contradictory records become available.
4. Original assignee: the player discovers Applicant 71-442-B.
5. Disposition: retain, return, or join.
6. Permanent record: the player receives a complete ending.

## Commercial boundary

Current published-plugin policy does not permit paid digital stories, printable packs, subscriptions, or freemium digital upsells. The initial app therefore:

- offers the entire case free;
- may provide free downloadable artifacts in a later release;
- does not advertise unrelated products;
- keeps future entitlement support outside the current player flow.

Physical Agency merchandise may be explored only after the story earns an audience and after a fresh policy review.

## Success signals

- Intake completion rate
- Percentage opening all four records
- Ending distribution
- Return conversations after completion
- Voluntary artifact downloads when added
- Qualitative evidence that players quote or share Agency language
