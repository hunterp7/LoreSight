# CRT bezel actions

- Inline CRT glass accepts a double-click/double-tap and requests fullscreen.
- The four lower bezel hit targets now map left-to-right to Imagine, Play or
  remix, Load your own, and Browse Library.
- A fifth Menu control occupies the right status area. The former status area
  is masked and a matching left lightbar is rendered by the shell.
- Menu actions are rendered inside the CRT surface and expose overflow actions
  (Create, Remix, Library, Controls, Settings, and Restart when available).
- The under-card custom toolbar now contains only the two native Apps SDK
  actions: Load your own and Browse Library.

Fullscreen suppresses physical bezel controls because the cabinet is not
visible; the native actions remain available through the host card.
