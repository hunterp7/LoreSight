# Graphics and Art Direction Brief

## Two related visual systems

Storyframe and The Agency should feel related through craft and mystery, but they are not the same brand surface.

### Storyframe Creator Studio

Fantasy: writing a living mystery book while shaping its causality in plain view.

Desired qualities:

- literary, tactile, intelligent, uncanny;
- an annotated manuscript crossed with an elegant evidence board;
- editorial marks, marginalia, chapter tabs, translucent tracing layers;
- critical moments feel pinned and definite;
- AI spans feel elastic, ghosted, and alive;
- canon feels authoritative without resembling enterprise data management;
- complex systems are progressively disclosed.

Avoid:

- generic SaaS dashboards;
- corkboard-and-red-string detective clichés;
- purple AI gradients;
- floating sparkle icons;
- node-graph spaghetti;
- skeuomorphic open-book pages that waste workspace;
- making every object look like a card.

Suggested material language:

- warm bone paper, graphite, carbon black, oxidized brass, faded editorial blue, restrained warning vermilion;
- serif display typography paired with a highly readable contemporary sans or mono annotation face;
- subtle paper grain and registration imperfections;
- motion resembling pages aligning, notes being underlined, and translucent layers settling into place.

### The Agency player world

Fantasy: auditing a cheerful institution that may have manufactured your identity.

Desired qualities:

- liminal institutional horror;
- retro administrative terminal and printed personnel artifacts;
- overconfident corporate service language;
- camp, absurd products, and mounting dread;
- original visual identity, not imitation.

Existing palette direction:

- sickly institutional green;
- acid yellow;
- faded paper;
- warning orange/vermilion;
- deep near-black terminal surfaces.

## First required asset set

Do not generate all assets at once. Establish and approve one anchor image before creating a family.

### Storyframe

1. One primary Creator Studio visual-direction mockup.
2. A semantic icon set for locked, secret, branch, open, unsaid, and never.
3. Critical-frame and elastic-span visual motifs.
4. Character-ledger portrait treatment and placeholder system.
5. Empty, loading, compiler-warning, and successful-publish states.

### The Agency

1. Agency emblem/mark.
2. Caseworker 43 portrait or employee-badge treatment.
3. Personnel Record A-17.
4. Elevator Transit Log 1974.
5. Childhood Starter Kit advertisement/package.
6. Original Assignee Memorandum.
7. Disposition certificate family.
8. Low-cost ambient texture and optional audio cover artwork.

## Asset metadata

Every asset receives:

- semantic asset key;
- world and version;
- creator/source;
- prompt or production notes;
- rights status;
- canon entities depicted;
- approval status;
- intended renderer and responsive crop guidance;
- alt text;
- revision lineage.

## Generation workflow

1. Generate exactly three visual directions for the same target.
2. Select or combine one direction.
3. Produce one anchor asset.
4. Review composition, originality, legibility, canon, and text accuracy.
5. Lock the art-direction prompt and negative constraints.
6. Generate the remaining family from the approved anchor.
7. Normalize size, format, transparency, and compression.
8. Test inside the actual interface before approval.

Do not trust image generation for exact artifact text. Generate the visual substrate, then render final typography and document content through HTML/CSS/SVG or another deterministic layout process.

## Originality boundary

Experiential references such as Omega Mart, Fallout terminals, Backrooms, Myst, Oregon Trail, or Dope Wars may inform qualities and mechanics. Do not copy their characters, logos, layouts, props, type treatments, UI, story facts, or recognizable visual assets.
