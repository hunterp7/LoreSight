# Product Decision Log

## Product

- Build an engine rather than a single game.
- The Agency is the first world pack and conformance test.
- Focus on turn-based narrative mechanics rather than graphically heavy gameplay.
- Support many structures: investigation, survival travel, trading/economy, relationships, puzzles, clues, and endings.

## Authoring

- Storyframe is a proprietary narrative keyframe language.
- Creators author critical moments; AI interpolates bounded connective play.
- Storyframe source is an intermediate/advanced representation, not the default UI.
- Creator Studio uses plain-language narrative questions and a visual story spine.
- **What the engine understood** exposes the semantic translation before approval.
- The Studio should feel like a choose-your-own-mystery book that the creator is writing.
- The Studio asks only questions whose answers materially change the game.

## Canon

- Creator has final authority over characters, setting, rules, history, and all canon.
- AI may propose but cannot publish canon.
- Canon states: LOCK, SECRET, BRANCH, OPEN, UNSAID, NEVER, plus proposal/deprecation lifecycle states.
- Unknown is not open. Intentionally unresolved questions may not be answered by AI.
- Published versions are immutable.

## Runtime

- Pure deterministic reducer owns simulation.
- AI is downstream of engine resolution.
- Generated details have turn, scene, session, or proposal scope.
- Authored fallbacks preserve playability.
- ChatGPT, web, CLI, and simulation hosts must run identical mechanics.

## Memory

- Universal recap surfaces: characters, places, clues/objects, and timeline.
- Character recap derives from a structured ledger.
- Player and creator see different projections.
- The AI Director receives only the smallest relevant recap.

## Visual direction

- Storyframe: living mystery manuscript, editorial layers, elegant evidence relationships.
- Agency: campy institutional surrealism and liminal horror.
- References are experiential only; all shipped IP and graphics must be original or licensed.

## Commercial and rights

- Do not depend on unlicensed “favorite IP” scenarios as the product wedge.
- Public/monetized worlds require original, public-domain, or licensed rights.
- Physical merchandise can be reconsidered only after audience and policy validation.
